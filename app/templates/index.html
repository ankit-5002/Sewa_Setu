Old Age Pension Portal

The Old Age Pension Scheme provides a monthly pension of Rs. 250 to eligible senior citizens aged 60 years and above who are residents of Purvanchal.

Application window is open. Applications accepted continuously without closing.

How to Apply

Follow the four simple steps below to submit your pension application.

Step 01

1. Mobile & OTP Verification

Verify your mobile number with OTP.

Step 02

2. Fill Application Form

Fill the application form (personal details, address, bank details).

Step 03

3. Upload Age Proof

Upload your age proof document (JPG or PDF, maximum 5 MB).

Step 04

4. Submit & Track

Submit the declaration. Note down your Application Number.
